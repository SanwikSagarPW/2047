Living Universe

Space around you is charted in real time as you fly. Earth is home sector 00:00. Real moons, asteroids and comets appear as you approach, and KORA pulls live knowledge about them from Wikipedia and Wikidata.

Real Spacecraft & Landing Sites

Real satellites orbit the planets: the ISS, Hubble, Tiangong, GPS, NavIC, Landsat, the James Webb Space Telescope, Chandrayaan-2, Mangalyaan, Juno and more. Glowing amber pins mark real landing sites at their true latitude and longitude — Apollo 11, Chandrayaan-3, Curiosity, Perseverance, Venera, Huygens and others. Fly close and press Q to scan them.

Beyond the Solar System

Open the Map and tap BEYOND. Golden arrows show real probes (Voyager 1 and 2, New Horizons), the Oort Cloud, nearby stars such as Alpha Centauri and Sirius, TRAPPIST-1, Betelgeuse, the Orion Nebula, the black hole Sagittarius A* and the Andromeda Galaxy. Plot a course and fly, or use a Fold Jump (15% fuel). Past Neptune, press H (or JUMP) for the Jump Drive: it cruises at high speed and stops near objects. B (or HOME) returns to Earth at any time, and R (or SHIP) brings the rover back to the ship. Distances are shrunk millions of times in the game; each scan shows the real distance in light-years.
